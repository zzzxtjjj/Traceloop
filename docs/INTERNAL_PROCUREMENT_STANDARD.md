# TradeLoop internal procurement comparison standard

Version: 1.0  
Effective date: 2026-10-25  
Owner: TradeLoop project lead

This is an internal workflow standard, not a platform policy or legal rule.

Before a supplier quote is treated as commercially comparable, record:

- the quoted product specification and unit;
- currency and unit price;
- MOQ and lead time;
- payment terms;
- applicable Incoterm;
- quote validity;
- whether packaging, freight, duty, tax, and other landed-cost items are included.

If one or more items are absent, the quote may still be stored, but the comparison must mark the
missing terms and must not describe the unit-price ranking as a total landed-cost ranking.
